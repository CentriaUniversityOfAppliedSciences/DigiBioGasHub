## Admin Documentation

The **Admin** section allows administrators to manage all aspects of the platform.  
Admins can access all admin features directly from the **navigation bar** by clicking the **“Admin”** button.

Admins have full control over materials, users, blog posts, companies, reports, and certificates.


### Features Overview

#### 1. Materials Management
Admins can manage materials.
- **Create** new materials  
- **Read** and view materials  
- **Delete** materials that are outdated or irrelevant  

#### 2. User Management
Admins can handle user-related operations.
- **Read** user profiles
- **Update** user information  
- **Delete** users when necessary  
- **Gift Membership** to users as part of promotions or rewards  

#### 3. Blog Posts Management
Admins have control over all articles and posts on the platform.
- **Publish** articles for public view  
- **Unpublish** articles temporarily  
- **Reject** articles that don’t meet content standards  

#### 4. Company Management
Admins oversee company profiles and their verification status.
- **Verify** legitimate companies  
- **Unverify** companies if needed  
- **Reject** company requests that don’t meet requirements  

#### 5. Reports & Analytics
Admins can view and analyze platform data and company performance.
- Access **visual reports** (e.g., **bar graphs**) displaying company statistics and performance metrics  

#### 6. Certificate Management
Admins manage certificates and their linkage to companies.
- **Create**, **update**, or **delete** certificates  
- Manage **company-related certificates** for validation and compliance  

### Access Level
Only users with **Admin privileges** can access these features.  
The admin dashboard and controls are available under the **Admin button in the top navigation bar**.
