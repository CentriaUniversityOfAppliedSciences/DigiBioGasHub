## DigiBioGasHubs Platform

DigiBioGasHubs platform is a digital marketplace designed to connect stakeholders in the biogas ecosystem. It enables users to offer or request biogas-related products and services, supporting collaboration and interaction between different actors. The platform aims to facilitate the development of biogas hubs (clusters) in various regions, promote the circular economy, and increase the share of renewable energy sources in the energy system. Key features include tools for marketplace listings, knowledge sharing, and ecosystem coordination.  

### Features

- **Marketplace**: Users can offer or request biogas and biomass products and services, enabling efficient matching between supply and demand.
- **Knowledge Base**: A wiki-style knowledge bank for sharing regulatory documents, guides, and best practices within the biogas community.
- **User and Company System**: Registration and management for individual users and organizations, supporting collaboration and networking.
- **Map View**: Visualize biogas-related offerings, companies, and services geographically to find resources and partners nearby.
- **Chat**: Integrated chat functionality for direct communication between platform users.
- **Blog**: A blog section for marketing, news, and sharing updates about the biogas sector.
- **Localization (I18N)**: Multi-language support for a broader user base.
- **Filters and Search**: Tools to filter and search marketplace listings efficiently.
- **Admin Functionalities**: Administrative tools for managing users, content, and platform operations.

*Planned features include subscriptions, agreements, and advanced search capabilities.*
