# General User Guide

Welcome to the platform! This guide explains **how to use the platform from start to finish**, from registration to managing companies, offers, articles, chat, subscriptions, and more.  

---

## 1. Getting Started

Before accessing all features, you must **register** or **login**.

### Navbar
The **navbar** provides quick access to all main features:
- Home  
- Marketplace  
- Map  
- Articles  
- Chat  
- My Contracts
- My Company  
- Reports
- Profile

You can also **change the platform language** from the navbar. Currently supported languages: **Finnish (fi), English (en), Swedish (sv)**.

---

## 2. Home Page

After logging in, the **Home Page** is your starting point. Here you can quickly see:

- **List of Offers**: Browse items or services available for sale.  
- **List of Articles**: Read informative articles posted in the platform.

### How to use:
- **View Offer Details**: Click any offer to see full details, including price, description, and location.  
- **Buy Offers**: If you like an offer click **Buy** and proceed.  
- **Contact Seller**: If you have questions, click **Contact Seller** to send a message.  
- **Read Articles**: Click any article title to read the full content.

---

## 3. Marketplace

The **Marketplace** is where you can browse, filter, and purchase offers.

### Accessing Marketplace
- Click **Marketplace** from the navbar.  
- All available offers will appear with images, descriptions, and prices.

### Adding an Offer
> Only available if you have a **verified company**. See the “My Company” section to learn how to verify your company.

1. Click **Add Offer** (top left of sidebar).  
2. Fill in the offer details.  
3. Submit the offer to make it visible in the marketplace.

### Filtering Offers
- **Basic filters**: Use the left sidebar to filter by categories, price, or other options.  
- **Advanced filters**: Click the **Advanced Filter** button on the right to filter by:
  - Price (cheapest to highest, or vice versa)  
  - Date (newest first or oldest first)  
  - Other criteria relevant to your needs

### Buying Offers
1. Click an offer you are interested in.  
2. Check the details and quantity.  
3. Click **Buy** and follow the checkout instructions.  
4. A purchase success notification will appear once completed. (Note: Actual payment integration is not yet implemented.)

---

## 4. Map

The **Map Page** provides a visual overview of biogas plants, offers, and companies locations.

### How to use:
- Hover over an icon to see a **short description**.  
- Click an offer icon to **view details** and **buy directly**.  
- Use the **left sidebar filters** to control which icons are visible on the map.

---

## 5. Articles

The **Articles Page** shows all **verified articles**.  

- Click any article to read it.  

---

## 6. Chat

The **Chat Page** allows users to communicate in **groups** or via **private messages**.

### Group Chat
- Join available **chat rooms** to discuss topics with multiple users.  
- Always follow **chat guidelines** to maintain a friendly environment.

### Private Chat
1. Go to **Profile → Settings → Chat Settings → Enable Private Chat → Save**.  
2. Go back to Chat and select a user from the **private chat list**.  
3. Send messages directly once private chat is enabled.

---

## 7. My Company

Manage your companies and access company-specific features.

### Company Sections
- **Verified**: Approved companies with full access  
- **Unverified**: Pending approval  
- **Rejected**: Not approved

### Adding a Company
1. Click **Add Company** (top left of sidebar).  
2. Fill out the company form with all required information.  
3. Submit the form. Your company will appear in the **unverified** list.

### Verified Company Features
- **Edit**: Update company information (requires re-verification if edited)  
- **Delete**: Remove the company entirely  
- **Offers**: Add, edit, or delete company offers  
- **Contracts**: Manage contracts related to the company  
- **Locations**: Add new company locations using **Add Location** button to make them appear on the map.
- **Terminals**: For logistics companies; view/add terminals  
- **Invite Members**: Invite users to join your company  
- **Users**: Manage members and their roles within the company  
- **Certificates**: Manage company certificates

---

## 8. Reports

The **Reports Page** displays analytical data about company and marketplace activities:

- Graphs showing **agreements, offers, and sales**  
- Insights into company performance and trends  
- Helps users make informed business decisions

---

## 9. Profile

Manage your personal account and settings. All options appear in the **left sidebar**.

### Edit
Update your personal details like name, email, or contact info.

### Settings
- Set default filters and preferences for **Marketplace** or **Map**.  
- Saves time by automatically applying preferred filters.

### Chat Settings
Enable or disable private chat with other users.

### API Keys
Generate API keys for platform services:
- **Personal API Key**: For individual use  
- **Company API Key**: For company use  

> Example: Sensor data or other external services can be connected using API keys.

### Blogs Management
- **Add Blog Post**: Create articles  
- **Upload PDF**: Upload documents in PDF format  
- **Sidebar Sections**:  
  - **Published**: Public articles  
  - **Pending Approval**: Articles waiting for approval  
  - **Drafts**: Newly created or edited articles (submit for approval)  
  - **Files**: Uploaded PDFs  
  - **Rejected**: Articles not approved  

### Manage Subscription
- **Buy Subscription**: Choose a plan and complete the purchase  
- **Cancel Subscription**: Cancel an active subscription via the list

### Change Password
- Currently **not functional**

### Delete Account
- Permanently delete your account if needed

---

## 10. Tips for New Users

- Always **verify your company** to unlock company-related features.  
- Set default filters in **Profile → Settings** for faster navigation.  
- Follow chat guidelines to maintain a friendly environment.  
- Use **API keys** to connect external services to the platform.  
- Explore **Marketplace, Map, and Articles** to understand how the platform works.
