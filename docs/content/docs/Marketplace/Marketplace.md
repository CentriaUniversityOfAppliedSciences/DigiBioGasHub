## Marketplace

The DigiBioGasHubs Marketplace is a core feature of the platform, providing a digital space where users can offer or request biogas and biomass products and services. It enables efficient matching between supply and demand within the biogas ecosystem. Key functions include:

- **Listing Offers and Requests**: Users can create, browse, and manage listings for biogas, biomass, and related services.
- **Filtering**: Advanced tools allow users to filter listings by criteria such as product type, price, and availability.
- **Geographical Map View**: Listings can be visualized on a map, helping users find resources and partners nearby.
- **Direct Communication**: Integrated chat functionality enables users to communicate directly regarding marketplace listings.
- **Company and User Profiles**: Organizations and individuals can showcase their offerings and credentials, supporting trust and collaboration.
- **Admin Tools**: Administrators can manage listings, moderate content, and oversee marketplace operations.
